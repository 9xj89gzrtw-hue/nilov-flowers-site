**Compiled:** 30.09.2026 · **Method:** z-ai web_search + page_reader (live site fetches) — 6 search queries + 12 competitor-site scrapes. Russian-language primary sources.
**Goal for Nilov:** "better than all SPb 2026" — translate into concrete, defensible differentiators (Section 3).

## 2. Content & UX patterns across the SPb niche

### 2.1 What almost everyone does (table-stakes now)
- **"Доставка за 1–2 часа"** promise (5cv, Гранд-Флора, Blumen Bar, РОМРОМ 60 мин, Flowwow 30 min).
- **24/7 ordering** (5cv, Гранд-Флора, Flowwow, Flawery, Blumen Bar). Exceptions: РОМРОМ 9–20, MoreFlowers 7–24.
- **Photo-before-send claim** as text (5cv, РОМРОМ, MoreFlowers opt-in checkbox). Nobody shows the photo *inside* the product UI as proof.
- **"Гарантия свежести"** as a slogan — universally claimed, never quantified (no day-count, no refund flow).
- **Catalog by species + occasion + recipient** — Розы/Пионы/Тюльпаны/Хризантемы/В коробке/Гортензии is the universal IA.
- **Tilda-based storefronts** for small studios (MoreFlowers, Blumen Bar, Marolli). Slow, blocky, look-alike.
- **Aggregator partner pages** on Flowwow (rating + review count + photo grid + 30-min SLA) is the dominant "small-shop discovery" UI.
- **Promocodes** for first order + email subscribers (5cv "20% off", Grand-Flora "промокоды", Flowwow "ПРИВЕТ" 1 000 ₽ / "PEONY15", MoreFlowers "15% на первый заказ").
- **Subscriptions** appearing (5cv, Blumen Bar, Magnolia — weekly bouquet).
- **Helium balloons + gifts** as cross-sell (5cv: 80 + 80).
- **Schema.org Florist + AggregateRating** in JSON-LD — 5cv has it perfect per-shop; others either skip or have it shallow.
- **Simplistic "Свадебные букеты" + "Авторские букеты"** sections — usually a flat catalog filter, no dedicated experience.
- **Open + close hours disclosed** (transparency about availability windows).
- **"Букеты в коробке"** as a flagship category (everyone offers hat-boxes; 5cv has 41 SKUs there).
- **СБП + Сплит (4 части)** BNPL — 5cv pioneered it, becoming table-stakes.
- **Telegram / WhatsApp** contact buttons (5cv, РОМРОМ — Telegram primary; everyone has WhatsApp).

### 2.2 What nobody does well (the gaps)
1. **No editorial scroll-storytelling on the storefront.** Catalog-first, marketing-journal-second. 5cv writes great journal posts but the storefront itself is a flat filterable catalog — no narrative arc, no florist-voice hero.
2. **No live "florist-photo-before-send" proof in the customer flow.** It's a checkbox (MoreFlowers) or a slogan (5cv, РОМРОМ) — nobody actually surfaces the proof photo as a UI artifact, with timestamp + florist's name, embedded into the order detail / thank-you page.
3. **No transparent freshness guarantee with day-count + refund flow.** Everyone says "свежие цветы"; nobody publishes "проживут 7 дней или вернём деньги" with a 1-click refund path. Гранд-Флора's "проверьте при получении" is closest but only at handover.
4. **No per-bouquet content density.** Cards show photo + price + (sometimes) rating. Nobody shows "what's inside this bouquet", "today's stem count", "florist who assembled it", "field-to-vase days", "care recipe".
5. **No real-time stock + delivery-zone UX.** Nobody shows "доступно к доставке в район X за Y минут" with live countdown.
6. **No design-language leadership.** Tilda templates dominate (MoreFlowers, Blumen Bar, Marolli). 5cv is the most modern (Next.js, editorial photography) — and it's still a transactional catalog with marketing polish, not an awwwards-grade editorial experience.
7. **No florist-as-author persona.** Nobody signs bouquets "Собрала Анна, флорист с 6 годами". The florist is invisible.
8. **No wedding / event micro-site.** "Свадебные букеты" is a flat category — no portfolio cases, no florist briefing flow, no inquiry-driven funnel.
9. **No corporate/B2B portal** (except РОМРОМ, barely). No "личный кабинет HR" with budget tiers, recurring orders, branded invoices.
10. **No care instruction UX** beyond a static page. No "what to do in the first 4 hours" micro-flow tied to the SKU.
11. **No eco/sustainability angle.** Nobody publishes "local vs. imported", "compostable packaging", "carbon-per-bouquet".
12. **No transparent zones + delivery-cost calculator.** Blumen Bar shows 3 flat fees (0/600/1500 ₽) — closest — but no per-district ETA map.
13. **Weak mobile commerce.** Tilda shops are slow on mobile; 5cv is best but not awwwards-level.
14. **No post-purchase "flower-life" tracker.** Nobody nudges the recipient on day 3/5/7 with care tips.
15. **No clear price-staircase or value-ladder UI.** Everyone shows "До 3 000 / 3 000–5 000 / 5 000–10 000 / от 10 000" (5cv does this best) — but nobody maps occasions to budget to recipient meaningfully.

## 3. What "better than all SPb 2026" means concretely for Nilov — 22 prioritized differentiators

> Format: [#] **differentiator** — why it wins (competitor closest to it).

**Tier A — Design-language & narrative (the awwwards-tier gaps):**
1. **Editorial scroll-storytelling hero** ("ОТКРЫВАЕМ ХОЛОДИЛЬНИК" → day-in-studio narrative) — only SPb shop with a magazine-grade scroll-driven hero. (5cv has the metaphor buried in a journal post; nobody has it on the storefront.)
2. **Design system 2 generations ahead** — dark-ink #0D120E + bone #F4EEE2 + bloom #E8607A + gold #C8A24A, Playfair/Golos/JetBrains — visibly editorial, not Tilda. (Closest: 5cv Next.js — but still a transactional design.)
3. **Florist-as-author persona system** — each bouquet card shows the florist who assembled it (name + tenure + photo). Nobody does this.
4. **"Один букет — два плана" gallery** — each SKU has a wide editorial shot + macro stem shot. (5cv has multiple angles; nobody has the editorial-macro pair as a system.)
5. **Live catalog as scroll-story** — categories revealed via scroll-stages (Холодильник → Стол флориста → Коробка → Доставка) — not a flat grid.
6. **Single-page content.json-driven vitrine** — every text on the storefront editable live from admin.html, zero redeploy. (5cv's journal isCMS-driven; nobody has a fully editable storefront.)

**Tier B — Trust & proof (the conversion-rate gaps):**
7. **Live florist-photo-before-send proof** — actual timestamped photo + florist name surfaced inside the order-detail page (not just an order-form checkbox like MoreFlowers or a slogan like 5cv/РОМРОМ).
8. **Transparent 7-day freshness guarantee + 1-click refund flow** — published day-count + visible refund UI in the thank-you page. (Nobody publishes a day-count; Гранд-Флора is closest with handover-check.)
9. **Per-bouquet content density card** — "что внутри": stem count, species, origin (Holland/Ecuador/local), field-to-vase days, care recipe. (Nobody does this.)
10. **Real-time delivery-zone ETA** — "доставим в Адмиралтейский за 38 минут" with live countdown. (Nobody has live ETA per district.)
11. **AggregateRating + 12 real reviews on site** — already done in W106; protect and amplify with photos of recipients' bouquets (with consent). (5cv has 465 Yandex reviews @ 5.0; nobody shows review-photos.)
12. **"Букет сегодня" live availability badge** — what's already assembled and shippable in <60 min, with florist's name. (Ромром has "Срочно за час" but no live counter.)

**Tier C — SLA & ops (the table-stakes-overdrive gaps):**
13. **24/7 ops + 1-hour SLA** (matching 5cv's 1–2h and beating РОМРОМ's 9–20 window).
14. **Free-delivery threshold published transparently** — "бесплатно от X ₽" with a clear calculator. (Blumen Bar shows 3 tiers; nobody has a free-threshold.)
15. **12 real SPb delivery zones with per-zone SLA** — already in W106; publish as interactive map (nobody has an interactive zones map).
16. **СБП + Сплит на 4 части + Плюс-баллы + ЮKassa** — match 5cv payment breadth, add instant-issuing receipts for B2B.
17. **Photo-on-delivery handover** — courier photographs bouquet at the door (with recipient consent) for proof-of-delivery. (Nobody does this in SPb.)

**Tier D — Adjacent segments (the growth gaps):**
18. **Wedding micro-site** — portfolio cases + florist briefing flow + inquiry funnel. ("Свадебные букеты" is flat-category everywhere; nobody has a dedicated experience.)
19. **B2B / corporate portal** — HR личный кабинет, recurring orders, branded invoices, bulk-tier pricing. (РОМРОМ has a section; nobody has a portal.)
20. **Subscription 2.0** — weekly bouquet + care-nudge sequence + florist-picks-next-week email. (5cv and Blumen Bar have basic subscriptions; no experiential layer.)
21. **Flower-life tracker** — day-3/5/7 SMS/email nudge with care tip + "продлить букет" upsell. (Nobody does this in SPb.)
22. **Sustainability provenance** — per-SKU "local vs. imported" + "compostable packaging" + "carbon footprint per bouquet". (Nobody does this; first-mover territory.)
